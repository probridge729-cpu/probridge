Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PROBRIDGE ("PROBRIDGE", "we", "us"), operated by [legal entity name], collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you visit www.probridge.co.in (the "Website") or contact us. It is published in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the "DPDP Act") and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules made under them.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us when you submit an enquiry, request a site visit or contact us:

  • Name
  • Mobile number
  • Email address (optional)
  • Your requirement: preferred location, budget, timeline and any other details you choose to share
  • The project or property you enquired about, and the page you sent the enquiry from
  • Your consent to be contacted

Information collected automatically. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. The Website stores a small value in your browser's session storage so the enquiry pop-up is not shown again during the same visit; it is deleted when you close the browser. Our hosting provider and the third-party services listed in section 4 may record technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, as part of providing their service.

Please do not share sensitive information such as bank details, passwords, Aadhaar numbers or health information through the Website forms.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry by call, SMS, WhatsApp or email.
  • To share property details, prices, availability and offers relevant to your requirement.
  • To arrange site visits and help you through booking, documentation and home-loan assistance.
  • To connect you with interior and home-maintenance service providers when you ask for those services.
  • To keep records of enquiries, prevent spam and misuse, and meet our legal obligations.

We use your information only for these purposes, on the basis of the consent you give when you submit a form.

3. Sharing with developers and service providers

When you enquire about a specific project, we may share your name, contact details and requirement with that project's developer or its sales team, so that they can confirm availability, arrange a site visit or complete a booking. When you ask for interior or home-maintenance services, we may share your details with the relevant service provider. We share only what is needed for the service you asked for.

We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information where required by law, by a court or by a government authority.

4. Third-party services used by the Website

  • Web3Forms: processes enquiry form submissions and delivers them to our email.
  • Zoho Mail: our business email service.
  • WhatsApp: if you choose to contact us through WhatsApp.
  • Google Maps: location maps on project pages.
  • Google Fonts and Unsplash: fonts and representative images loaded by your browser.
  • [Hosting provider]: hosts the Website.

These services process data under their own privacy policies.

5. How long we keep your information

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond to you and to assist with your property journey, and for up to [24 months] after our last contact with you, unless a longer period is required by law. After that, we delete or anonymise it. If you withdraw your consent, we stop contacting you and delete your details, except where we must keep them by law.

6. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act, you can:

  • Ask for a summary of the personal information we hold about you and how we use it.
  • Ask us to correct, complete or update it.
  • Ask us to delete it.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time. This does not affect anything done before you withdrew it.
  • Nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf in case of death or incapacity.
  • Raise a grievance with us, and then with the Data Protection Board of India if it is not resolved.

To use any of these rights, email info@probridge.co.in or contact the Grievance Officer below.

7. Security

We use reasonable security practices to protect your information, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections and restricted access to enquiry emails. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will act promptly on any breach as required by law.

8. Children

The Website is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

9. Links to other websites

The Website may link to developer websites, the RERA portal or other sites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices; please read their policies.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.
